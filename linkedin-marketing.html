Choose your plan

Buyers research before they reach out. They open your LinkedIn profile, scroll your posts, and decide in under a minute whether you are the expert they need.

Most founders and senior professionals know their field deeply, yet their profile says very little about it. Posting is irregular, ideas stay in their heads, and the people who could become customers or partners never get a reason to remember them. That is a visibility problem, and it has a fix.

LinkedIn Marketing & Branding at Blue Sky is a campaign built around you. We start with who you want to reach, your ideal customer profile (ICP), and then build a steady presence around them: content that shows what you know, a profile that backs it up, and active outreach that puts you in front of the right people.

We make you the thought leader in your field

Regular posting is what separates a known expert from a silent one. We write your content in your voice, design motion posters and visuals that stop the scroll, and publish at the times your audience is online. Over weeks, your profile starts to look like what it is: the home of someone who really knows the subject.

When the right people see you teach, share and show up every week, they stop asking whether to talk to you. They ask when.

We build visibility around your ICP and their network

Content alone doesn't reach everyone who matters. We connect with potential customers and partners in your target network, engage with their posts, and bring them to your profile, so the audience you are building is made of people who can actually buy, refer or collaborate.

We turn attention into conversations and customers

At the higher plans we go beyond posting. We reach out, start conversations, comment on the right profiles to pull in traction and impressions, and on our top plan, speak with prospects on your behalf and book meetings straight into your calendar. You get the outcome without living on LinkedIn yourself.

We track what is working, so every month gets sharper

On the Stratosphere plan we also track how leads are created and how you use them, so we know what to double down on next. Growth becomes a system you can learn from, not guesswork.

Common questions

Why do the plans have minimum commitments?

LinkedIn growth compounds. A profile needs consistent posting and engagement over time to build real traction, so Horizon needs 6 months, while Altitude and Stratosphere need 3 months to show considerable growth.

What is the difference between the plans?

Horizon builds your content presence. Altitude adds more posts and active outreach. Stratosphere runs LinkedIn end to end: daily posting, profile makeover, outreach, commenting, conversations on your behalf, meeting booking and lead tracking.

Do I have to be active on LinkedIn myself?

Not on the Stratosphere plan. We handle posting, outreach, engagement and conversations, and book meetings straight into your calendar so you only need to show up for them.

What do you track?

On Stratosphere, we track how leads are created and how you use them, so each month we know what to repeat and what to change.
